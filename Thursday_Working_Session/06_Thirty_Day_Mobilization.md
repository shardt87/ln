## Sequencing logic worth stating

1. Quality gates before volume. OEM outreach, customer adaptations of the labor proof and the NRG language revision all sit upstream of any acceleration, exactly as the brief's follow-ups direct (E2). The plan never asks a play to move faster than its gate.
2. Baseline before playbooks. The August 28 baseline is the plan's critical path. Every downstream artifact (playbooks, sizing, readout) consumes it. If it slips, the September update slips in quality even if the date holds, so slippage on the baseline escalates to Mark on August 28, not later.
3. External timing is flagged, not assumed. NRG's response and Southern Power's availability are outside Southwire's control. The plan treats them as inputs when they arrive, and the September readout is written to stand without them if needed.

## Out of scope for these 30 days

Per the brief's parking lot (D4): TAM detail, product mapping by plant zone, project-level lists, nuclear deep dives, substation white space, SKU-level capacity planning, channel compensation exceptions, CRM attribution mechanics, collateral redesign, org design and headcount. Each has a capture line in file 07 with an owner and a return window.
