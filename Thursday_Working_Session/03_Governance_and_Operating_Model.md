## Operating posture (the owners' own rules)

1. **Business development writes the strategy; the businesses keep execution.** "We're just the business developers... putting the strategy together so that we can expand our current foothold... I'm not trying to get in your way or tell you what you're doing" (T p.15). The verticals keep their product knowledge, relationships, and delivery.
2. **Sales executes; sales does not dictate.** "They are our sales execution arm and not someone who dictates the strategy" (T p.8). Meetings without agendas that consume owner time get declined ("if you get a 30-minute meeting and there's not really an agenda... I'm going to save my time," T p.2).
3. **Utility and Renewables stay verticals.** Utility operates as the legacy vertical and Renewables as its own vertical; both strategies get incorporated into the one overarching PowerGen strategy rather than replaced by it (T pp.11-12).
4. **Quality gates on everything outbound.** Messages Mike drafts require approval until quality is consistent; marketing material carries copyright and non-forwardable protection; the energy deck is not handed to third parties to forward at will (T pp.8-10, 14).
5. **Channel peace is a design constraint, not an afterthought.** No unprompted direct offering; the hedge model (owner buys material, EPC's contractor installs) is the sanctioned direct framing; OEM route decided per account by how the account already buys; big accounts stay with distribution (T pp.3, 7).
6. **Relationship-led, evidence-backed.** The outreach machine runs on genuine relationships (golf, Formula E, mentorship) and defensible material; every claim in customer material is either owned or labeled adjacent (T pp.3-5).
