## Session rules (the owners' own)

- **Rabbit holes get parked by name.** "If we're going down a rabbit hole... we call it what it is... park that and say, that's parked... in the rabbit hole zone." Park, log, return date, continue.
- **Guardrails against the moon.** "We all know we got to put some carbrails around it. Otherwise, we will end up talking about building a power plant in the moon."
- **Three words written down is failure.** The session exists so that two hours don't go by with nothing on paper; every block ends with something written.
