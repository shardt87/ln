## What the map exposes

Stated neutrally, as the evidence supports:

1. Strengths. Established Utility and Renewables positions are asserted in the brief and are the foundation the strategy builds from (S1 para 7). Owner-level conversations have started in the IPP lane with two named counterparties and one named target (C1-C6). OEM targeting has a named owner and an external advisor (C7, C8).
2. Unclear handoffs. No documented rule exists for who leads when an owner pursuit touches an account another business or channel already serves. The brief defers the detailed rules (D4) while the pursuits are already live. This is the sharpest near-term risk on the map.
3. White space. Repower/brownfield and prefab appear in the portfolio hypothesis with no underlying activity evidence. They are strategy concepts today, not businesses.
4. Channel risk. Owner-direct dialogue (NRG, Southern Power) proceeds while distribution and agent rules for PowerGen remain undefined. B5 states the mitigating principle; it is not yet adopted policy.
5. Visibility gap. No consolidated pipeline, account list or activity view exists (D2). Every business holds its own picture. The baseline workstream is the fix and must land by August 28 per the brief's own plan.

No statement here characterizes any internal team as failing or territorial. The evidence contains no basis for such a claim, and the gaps above are structural, not behavioral.
