THE ROUTES
Nobody gets bypassed: the hedge, not the bypass.
The sanctioned direct framing
The owner buys material direct; the electrical contractor the EPC hires installs it. The owner de-risks schedule and change orders without displacing anyone. “It's like even hedging.”
Never signal direct unprompted
“We can piss off distribution by going directly… we can piss off an EPC by going directly to an IPP.” Open to discuss if the customer raises it.
Route OEMs by how they buy
First look behind the curtain: what runs through distribution stays there. Direct only where the account already buys direct (Powell is the example).
Big accounts stay with distribution
“Those big accounts, leave it to distribution to figure it out.”
Relationships open doors
Golf, Formula E in Austin, mentorship. A champion forwards the leave-behind; a 30-minute call precedes any meeting; the visit is one hour, experts, no sales deck.
Transcript pp.3-5, 7. Channel peace is a design constraint of the strategy, not an afterthought.
