THE THESIS
Time to power is an electrical execution problem, argued in the customer’s economics.
THREE EXECUTION RISKS
Labor demand pressure
Prefab, kitting, and a yard near site move hours off the jobsite.
Material and installation lead
Early material planning against the project schedule.
Fragmented coordination
One coordinated electrical execution plan; change orders are the budget’s biggest bleed.
THE ECONOMIC ARGUMENT
The LCOE denominator
Energy delivered sits under the levelized cost of energy. Conductor temperature rating changes deliverable amps; that lands on an IPP’s bottom line.
One percent, high coefficient
“I know we are one percent, but if we fail, it could cost much more.” Small share of capex, outsized execution risk: that is the seat at the planning table.
