## Red-team questions for the recommended model

1. If PowerGen cannot compel timely functional decisions, is “federated” simply a new name for today’s coordination problem?
2. If existing account owners keep relationship control and businesses keep all execution authority, what exact decisions make PowerGen accountable rather than advisory?
3. Could one shared pipeline double-count owner influence, OEM scope, and field orders even with a common project ID?
4. Will an enterprise-priority label distort capacity allocation before value and margin are validated?
5. Is OEM/modular truly a repeatable platform opportunity, or a set of custom projects with weak conversion economics?
6. Does the Time to Power position depend on prefab capabilities Southwire does not yet control at scale?
7. Could an early direct OEM push weaken distributor or agent relationships that are required for field conversion?
8. Are new-build gas capture and OEM/modular embedding distinct enough to resource separately, while EPC/OE influence and prefab remain cross-cutting route/capability elements?
9. Which outcome after 90 days would prove that a standalone vertical is necessary, and which would prove it is not?
10. What will Southwire deliberately stop or defer so the initial portfolio is real rather than additive?

## Evidence required before changing the recommendation

The recommendation should be reconsidered if any of the following is established:

- A sponsor will not grant cross-business convening and escalation authority.
- Existing businesses cannot commit named functional resources.
- Current account/channel commitments prevent coordinated pursuit of the selected lighthouse projects.
- Product, capacity, qualification, or economics make the selected validation priorities unattractive.
- Customer discovery disproves the early-influence and Time to Power proposition.
- The majority of priority work can be executed within one existing business without recurring cross-business handoffs.
- A standalone vertical has a documented P&L, resources, customer-transition plan, and benefits that materially exceed the federated pilot.

## Thursday answer discipline

For every question, record one of four outcomes:

- **Decided:** answer, owner, and effective date are clear.
- **Modified:** a different answer is chosen and documented.
- **Deferred:** owner, evidence needed, and decision date are named.
- **Rejected:** the issue is outside the pilot and will not consume 30-day resources.
