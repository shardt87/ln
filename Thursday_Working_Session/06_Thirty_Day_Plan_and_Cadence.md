## Risks and controls

| Risk | Early warning | Control |
|---|---|---|
| The strategy slips past the 30 days | Thursday produces "three words written down" | The three non-negotiables and per-block written outputs; daily pre-work hour |
| A call-quoted number escapes into a customer or leadership document | Any figure without a written source | Numbers discipline: internal-only until confirmed; external firm for defensible sizing |
| Channel damage from direct signaling | Distribution or an EPC reacts to an outreach | The guardrails in 03; hedge-model framing only; route per account by existing buying behavior |
| OEM outreach quality embarrasses the effort | Misspelled or unapproved sends | The approval gate stays until quality is consistent; role-play before visits |
| Marketing produces catalog-style or borrowed material | Draft visuals look like product pages; any uncredited diagram | Colorito alignment; application-story standard; copyright and non-forwardable marks |
| The monthly call drifts into status theater | No decisions or asks on the agenda | Agenda fixed to the four questions; gaps-and-risks section carries the asks |
