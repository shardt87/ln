# Deck Review — Southwire Power Generation Teaser (for IPPs / NRG)

**Reviewed:** `Power_Gen_Teaser_Southwire_Final.pdf` (SWR-PG-TEASER-V Rev 07, 14 pages) and
`PowerGen_VP_UseCases_Rev03_2.pdf` (9 pages).
**Deliverable:** rebuilt teaser (`Southwire_PowerGen_Teaser_IPP_Rev08.pptx` / `.pdf`) executing every item below.

---

## Overall verdict

The strategy underneath this deck is genuinely strong — arguably best-in-class for a cable
manufacturer selling to an IPP. The NRG-specific homework (5.4 GW GE Vernova/Kiewit-TIC newbuild,
13 GW LS Power fleet, TEF/PJM uprates, data-center colocation LOIs), the IRR/LCOE framing, the
"field-engineer what is unique, prefabricate what repeats" thesis, the honest disclaimers, and the
"One hour. One site walk." ask are all excellent. The execution of the artifact, however, undermines
the credibility the content earns: empty slides, cropped screenshots, wrong page numbers, mixed
templates, and copy-paste kicker errors. A buyer who is being sold *execution discipline* will read
the deck itself as evidence.

## Critical issues (fixed in rebuild)

1. **Two completely empty slides** — "Combined Cycle Full Solution" (p8) and "Sustainability
   Partner" (p12) are title-only placeholders. In a teaser, an empty slide is worse than no slide.
   → Cut. Their intent is absorbed into the proof and company slides.
2. **Wrong kicker labels on four slides** — pages 8, 10, 12, 13 all carry the kicker
   "PROOF, FROM AN ADJACENT MARKET · FUEL CELL DEVELOPER, NOT NAMED" over unrelated content
   (value proposition, sustainability, company overview). Copy-paste artifact. → Every kicker
   rewritten to match its slide.
3. **Broken pagination** — footers read "6 of 8", "7 of 8", "8 of 8" in a 14-page deck; most pages
   have no footer at all. → One consistent footer system, correct numbering, single Rev tag.
4. **Cropped / cut-off content** — the LCOE table on "The Problem" (p4) is cut mid-row; the
   disclaimer paragraph on the 4,400→880 slide (p7) runs off the right edge mid-word
   ("subtract the pr…"). These are the two most analytical slides — exactly where an IPP's eye
   will go. → Both rebuilt natively (no screenshots), full text visible.
5. **Typos/grammar on a money slide** — "Three things push the date and influences  IRR/LCOE"
   (agreement error + double space); "Time (Or Speed) to power" headline. → Rewritten.
6. **The SK-120 poster crammed into a slide** (p9) — a 34-inch poster at slide size is illegible
   and its whitespace collapses. → The six schedule stats promoted to a native stat-tile slide;
   the full poster moved to a full-bleed appendix page where it can actually be read.
7. **Template whiplash** — the deck mixes at least four visual systems (dark hex brand pages,
   white minimal pages, a Verdana-ish process slide, raw-screenshot pages). → One system:
   dark cover/close sandwiching light content pages, one type family, one copper accent,
   hexagon numbering motif carried throughout (echoes the brand texture).

## Strategic feedback (also executed)

8. **Too long for a teaser.** 14 pages with duplicated value-prop content (pp10 duplicates the VP
   deck). A teaser earns a meeting; it doesn't close scope. → 11 content slides + 1 appendix.
9. **The NRG hook was buried.** The strongest slide ("Doubling down on generation…", p3) sat
   behind two generic pages, and page 2 crams the NRG paragraph into a corner in 8pt text.
   → "Why now" is now slide 2, with the four program moves and "where cable decides" for each.
10. **Internal enablement content leaked into an external artifact.** "Value Proposition
    Statement" and "One Proposition. Four Use Cases." (from the internal VP deck, still footed
    "Internal concept draft") read as being sold *at*, not spoken *to*. → Reframed as
    "What this looks like inside NRG's program" — each use case mapped to a concrete NRG
    program element (2029 COD fleet, 18 acquired plants, TEF/PJM uprates, DC colocation).
11. **The Owner's Equation belongs in the teaser.** The VP deck's LCOE/IRR levers slide is the
    single best IPP-language asset in either file and was missing from the teaser. → Added as
    slide 4, rebuilt natively.
12. **Proof discipline is a differentiator — keep it.** The honest qualifiers ("modeled, not
    observed", "gross, not net", "illustrative until validated against your labor data") are rare
    and credible. → Retained verbatim in clean, fully-visible fine print.
13. **Contact/CTA strength retained** — cover and "One hour. One site walk." close are kept
    essentially as-is; they were the best-designed pages in the file.
