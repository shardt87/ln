Zone 01 of 07
230 kV switchyard and lines
01  Grid connection
The plant's output leaves through step-up transformers and a breaker-and-a-half 230 kV switchyard. Where the circuits run underground, HV cable rises to outdoor terminations; overhead conductor carries them to the grid.
WHERE IN THE CAMPUS
1
HV XLPE transmission cable, 230 kV
Underground circuits from the step-up transformers and the CCS and modular-yard ties: Cu conductor, XLPE, metallic moisture barrier, HDPE jacket.
2
Overhead conductor (ACSR / ACSS)
The 230 kV lines leaving the terminal towers, and the switchyard bus jumpers.
3
Bare copper grounding conductor
The station ground grid, rods and risers under the yard and every structure.
4
Type TC-ER control cable, shielded
Breaker, CT / VT and relay circuits in the yard trenches to the relay house.
5
MV-105 shielded power cable
15-35 kV station-service and auxiliary feeders at the yard edge.

Zone 02 of 07
Gas turbines, generators, steam turbine
02  Power island
Three H-class gas turbines and a steam turbine share one hall. Every auxiliary that keeps them turning, from lube oil to fuel valves to the static starters, is fed and controlled by cable.
WHERE IN THE CAMPUS
1
ARMOR-X® MC-HL 15 kV, red PVC
13.8 kV feeders to auxiliary drives through the Class I, Div. 2 turbine areas, laid in tray with no conduit.
2
ARMOR-X® MC-HL 600 V, black PVC
Motor and power circuits on the turbine enclosures, lube-oil and fuel skids.
3
MV-105 15 kV, EPR 133%
Switchgear to unit transformers, boiler feed pumps and the static starters.
4
VFD cable, symmetrical grounds, shielded
Drives to pumps and fans: controls noise and reflected-wave stress.
5
Instrumentation cable (PLTC / ITC)
Turbine and HRSG control loops, transmitters and thermocouples.

Zone 03 of 07
Inlet chilling, cooling water, buildings
03  Balance of plant
Around the power island, the balance of plant keeps it running. Here, the inlet-chilling plant: six water-cooled chillers, cooling-water pumps, an e-house and a 12-cell tower, all fed, controlled and protected by cable.
WHERE IN THE CAMPUS
1
MV-105 5 kV and 15 kV
13.8 and 4.16 kV feeders to the chiller compressors, cooling-water pumps and station transformers.
2
Type TC-ER 600 V power
Motor-control-center and panel feeders out of the e-house and across the racks.
3
SIMpull® THHN / XHHW-2 building wire
Lighting, receptacles and HVAC in the chiller, water-treatment, control and admin buildings.
4
Type TC-ER control, multiconductor
Pump, valve and level interlocks between the e-house and the process.
5
Fire alarm and life-safety cable
Detection and notification circuits in the plant buildings and e-houses.
