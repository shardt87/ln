01 — The job
Every circuit.
Every pull.
On schedule.
A 1,700 MW combined-cycle campus with carbon capture, battery storage and modular power is more than five million feet of cable, nearly six thousand circuits, and hundreds of pulls that all have to happen in the right order, in outage windows, around live plant.
This brochure follows that cable through a project, from the schedule to the energised plant, and then walks the campus zone by zone: what makes installation different there, and what Southwire brings to make it safer, faster and cleaner. Every image and number comes from one 3D model of the plant and its cable schedule.

At the shop.
Modular-yard expansion: spine module 5 of 9 comes down on its bents, modules 1-4 already set from the MOD-EH e-house; electricians on the boom lift take the coiled tails.
