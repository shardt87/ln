# Customer story for the Nov 11 training (one slide)

Fill in what you can; anonymise anything sensitive (for example "an IPP in Texas" instead of the name). Five minutes is enough.

1. **The project:** type (combined cycle, peaker, engines, BTM data centre, upgrade), size (MW), state or region, greenfield or brownfield.
2. **Who bought:** owner type (IPP, utility, co-op, data-centre developer), who specified (owner's engineer), who placed the order (EPC, package builder, distributor).
3. **What we supplied:** product families and rough quantities (feet, reels), any services (cut-to-length, SIMpull, pre-terminated, spec support).
4. **The problem:** what the customer was worried about (schedule, supply, spec, hazardous areas, installation crew).
5. **What made the difference:** why us; one sentence the customer would say.
6. **The result:** delivered on time, days saved, follow-on orders, any number you can share.
7. **Can we show it:** internal only, or OK with names?
