Where we win

  • Standard blocks = standard kits. Every block is the same: cut-to-length, tagged lead sets per genset, shipped per block.
  • Paralleled pulls. ~1,600 lead pulls at 366 MW: SIMpull and paralleled reels cut the pull time.
  • Factory side. 1 GW/yr of genset production in Texas: harness and enclosure wire in the factory (bucket 1).
  • Reel logistics on a 30-acre site: staged delivery, reel return, job-site services.
  • MV pre-terminated with elbows: faster energisation of each block.

Ask on the call

  • Genset output voltage, kW per unit, gensets per block.
  • Collection voltage: 13.8 or 34.5 kV? Duct bank, tray or direct burial?
  • Who buys the cable: ERock, the EPC, or the utility owner (El Paso Electric for Meta)?
  • Copper or aluminium on the leads and collection?
  • Factory wiring inside the enclosure: who supplies it today?
  • Schedule: Meta (366 MW, May 2027 target), Microsoft San Jose, the pipeline.
