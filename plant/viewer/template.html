Start-up path, GT-1 (SK-3X1-04)

The 230 kV bus back-feeds GSU-1 through D1. The UAT-1 tap sits on the GSU side of GCB-1, so with GCB-1 open the grid energises UAT-1 and 13.8 kV bus 1 in R1. Bus 1 feeds the LCI-1 input transformer, and LCI-1 drives GTG-1 as a motor on the generator side of GCB-1. GCB-1 closes at synchronisation.

Assumed ratings

Generators 21 kV. GSUs 21/230 kV, one per generator. UATs 21/13.8 kV. T4-A/B 13.8/4.16 kV. LCT-A/B, T-R2A-C, T-R3, T-R4-1..4 and T-WT 13.8/0.48 kV. MVA, impedance and fault levels are not calculated.

Switchyard

Breaker-and-a-half, 5 diameters x 3 breakers: 15 positions, 9 installed. D1 GTG-1 + Line 1, D2 GTG-2 + Line 2, D3 STG + GTG-3. D4 (BESS / modular yard) and D5 (CCS) are future; D6 (green H2 import) is an adjacent-market extension.

Model heights drawn to one scale, with the sheet-13 reference for each class. The order HRSG < ACC < HRSG stack < stripper < absorber < absorber stack follows real plants.

How the model was built

Plan positions come from the vector geometry of sheets 01, 02 and 05. On sheet 01 the frame maps exactly to the 2,420 x 1,920 ft compound (3.115 ft per PDF point); on sheet 05 the R1 plan is rotated with plant north to the right (4.08 pt per ft). Routes are the drawn centrelines lifted to their tiers: MV tray EL +36, control tray EL +42, IPB about EL 20.

How it was checked

verify.py matches every drawn shape to a model item within 0.6 ft, reproduces the sheet-13 heights, recomputes the sheet-09 clearance screen and hook margins from the model, and tests every pair of parts for clashes. The Verification tab shows the latest run.

What the checks corrected

They caught GT combustor cans above the EL 38 envelope (which cut the GT hook margin to 9.7 ft instead of 11), an ACC steam riser rising through the fans, and three misread shapes: the filter-house stair tower, the hoist landing with truck access, and the gallery-door forklift apron (sheet 10).

Simplifications

Equipment is built from boxes, cylinders, cones, A-frames and lofts. Cabinet heights, drum sizes, transformer radiators, gantries and structural steel are typical where no drawing gives them, and are tagged typical. Conceptual illustration: not engineered, not for construction.
