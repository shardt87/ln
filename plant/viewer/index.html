Start-up path, GT-1 (SK-3X1-04)

230 kV bus back-feeds GSU-1 through D1. The UAT-1 tap sits on the GSU side of GCB-1, so with GCB-1 open the grid energises UAT-1 and 13.8 kV bus 1 in R1. Bus 1 feeds the LCI-1 input transformer; LCI-1 drives GTG-1 as a motor on the generator side of GCB-1. GCB-1 closes at synchronisation.

Assumed ratings

Generators 21 kV. GSUs 21/230 kV, one per generator. UATs 21/13.8 kV. T4-A/B 13.8/4.16 kV. LCT-A/B, T-R2A-C, T-R3, T-R4-1..4 and T-WT 13.8/0.48 kV. MVA, impedance and fault levels are not calculated.

Switchyard

Breaker-and-a-half, 5 diameters x 3 breakers: 15 positions, 9 installed. D1 GTG-1 + Line 1, D2 GTG-2 + Line 2, D3 STG + GTG-3. D4 (BESS / modular yard) and D5 (CCS) are future; D6 (green H2 import) is an adjacent-market extension.

Model heights after Rev 12, drawn to one scale (SK-3X1-13). The order HRSG < ACC < HRSG stack < stripper < absorber < absorber stack follows real plants.

How this model was built

Plan positions were read from the vector geometry of sheets 01 and 02, where the drawing maps exactly to the 2,420 x 1,920 ft compound (3.115 ft per PDF point). Every footprint lands on the round-foot coordinates of the original coordinate model. Cable, tray, bus and pipe routes are the drawn centrelines, lifted to their stated tiers (MV tray EL +36, control tray EL +42, IPB EL 20).

Heights

From the hall section (SK-3X1-09), the dimension review (SK-3X1-12) and the height comparison (SK-3X1-13). Items without a stated height use typical values and are tagged typical. Conditional items are tagged conditional.

Simplifications

Equipment is shown as boxes, cylinders and A-frame prisms. HRSG internals, crane, IPB phases, pipe sizes and conductor bundles are not modelled. Filter-house frames, stair towers, rack bents and ACC columns are representative.

Status

Conceptual illustration. Not engineered. Not for construction. All optional counts, sizes and ratings are assumptions, and each optional system is Decision Required before cable is scheduled.
