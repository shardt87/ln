## Evidence discipline (applies to every file here)

1. Every claim is labeled **Source Fact**, **Inference**, **Recommendation**, or **Open Question**, or it does not ship.
2. A modeled candidate is not an opportunity. Relationship status is not a commercial stage.
3. Unreconciled figures (≈$1.8B directional TAM, $53M annual potential, $9.7M quote activity, 30 vs. 31 Tier 1) stay internal and labeled *directional* until Finance/Sales Operations reconciliation signs off.
4. The 4,400 → ≈880 labor-hour benchmark is an adjacent case; it is not used externally until verified for baseline, scope, and Southwire contribution.
5. No authority, account transfer, capacity promise, pricing, or route change is asserted in these documents; those remain decisions of the accountable businesses and the sponsor.
