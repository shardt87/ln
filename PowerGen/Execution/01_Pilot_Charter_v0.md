# Enterprise PowerGen Interim Pilot Charter (v0 draft)

**Pilot window:** August 21 – November 18, 2026  |  **Ratification:** Amber, late September 2026
**Status:** Draft for Marc's approval. This charter has no force until D-01 through D-03 are marked
Decided in `00_Decision_Log.md` and Marc confirms sponsor language.

## Purpose

Operate Power Generation as a **federated horizontal enterprise platform** for 90 days: one strategy,
one project view, one pursuit discipline, and one escalation path — while every existing business keeps
its product, pricing, capacity, manufacturing, fulfillment, channel, warranty, P&L, and booked-revenue
authority, and every existing account owner keeps relationship execution unless accountable leaders
approve a change.

## Scope (per D-01 recommended default; confirm against decision log)

| Lane | Pilot treatment |
|---|---|
| New-build gas project capture | Immediate 30-day market validation |
| OEM/modular platform embedding | Immediate 30-day market validation |
| Repower/brownfield | Conditional — activates only with a named customer sponsor, project, route, and repeatable hypothesis |
| Utility and Renewables | Foundation mapping (current-state submission required) |
| Data Center/BTM; nuclear/SMR | Evidence mapping only; no commercial activation without a separate sponsor gate |
| EPC/OE | Cross-cutting influence route, not a separate end market |
| Prefab, kitting, application engineering, supply planning, field support | Enabling capabilities, not standalone market plays |

## PowerGen may (if approved)

- Maintain the common Power Generation account and project view.
- Propose the stage taxonomy and evidence requirements; govern the approved taxonomy after Marc's
  approval (CRM/operations owns system fields and data control).
- Convene cross-business teams for lighthouse pursuits and require a complete pursuit charter before
  enterprise-priority status.
- Recommend portfolio and resource tradeoffs; coordinate the integrated customer narrative.
- Escalate unresolved account, capacity, route, and credit conflicts; report progress, decisions,
  risks, and evidence gaps to leadership.

## PowerGen may not

- Reassign an existing account unilaterally.
- Quote or approve pricing outside existing authority.
- Promise capacity, lead time, engineering performance, or delivery.
- Change an established distributor, agent, or direct-account route unilaterally.
- Commit a product team, manufacturing site, or service group without approval.
- Take booked revenue, P&L, fulfillment, quality, or warranty responsibility from an existing business.
- Present a target, relationship, or specification influence as an award.

## Escalation

Unresolved route, credit, or ownership conflicts follow the conflict-resolution protocol in
`../Thursday_Working_Session/03_Operating_Model_Options.md`. If D-07 is approved, the sponsor
responds within the proposed 48-hour target. Escalation owner: Marc (proposed).
