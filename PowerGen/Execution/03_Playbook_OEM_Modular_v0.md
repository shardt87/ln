**Status:** Working draft. All accounts in the Rev89C model are **candidates** (S09), not opportunities.

## 1. Customer problem hypothesis

OEMs and skid/modular builders assembling gas power packages need compatible, repeatable,
factory-ready cable architecture — approved cable families, package BOMs, pre-cut/labeled kits,
interface data — plus a reliable supply plan. *(Hypothesis from S04/S09 model; validate with actual
buyer, boundary, and stage evidence per account.)*

## 2. Southwire offer hypothesis

Factory-to-field cable architecture: compatibility engineering (VFD/motor, hazardous-area, fire,
shielding, fiber, bend radius, pulling, termination), approved cable families, standardized package
BOMs, pre-cut/labeled kits, cable schedules, and interface data — linked to field-order conversion
through the EPC/contractor/channel lane.

## 3. Two linked lanes (per S09 recommendation; never merged into one pipeline record)

| Lane | Who acts | What converts |
|---|---|---|
| OEM/package approval | OEM/package engineering approves and specifies; may buy factory scope | Factory-wired scope, package cable |
| Field conversion | EPC, contractor, distributor, or channel buys bulk field scope | Field cable demand pulled through by specification |

One project ID links the lanes; records stay separate; no double counting.

## 6. Discovery standard (per account)

1. Which packages does the account actually build; what cable scope is factory-wired vs. shipped loose.
2. Who approves cable families and interfaces; who issues the purchase order; on what cycle.
3. Package specification, one-lines, cable schedule, and terminal data availability.
4. Current cable suppliers and channel arrangements (respect existing agreements; note conflicts for
   escalation, don't work around them).
5. Repeatability: is this a platform with standard BOM potential or one-off custom work
   (red-team question 5)?
6. Field-scope path: which EPC/contractor/channel converts the field demand this account's packages create.

## 7. Route guardrails specific to this play

- **Direct OEM pursuit is not assumed.** Marc's channel-conflict caution (S07) applies; an early
  direct push must not weaken distributor/agent relationships needed for field conversion
  (red-team question 7). Account-specific route decisions belong to commercial owners.
- No assumption of owner-direct or OEM-direct field scope.
- Quote authority stays with the existing business/account process; named approver TBD per account.
