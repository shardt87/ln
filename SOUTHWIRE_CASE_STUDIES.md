## Part 1 — Southwire as the featured customer

### 1. Google Cloud / Avvale / NIMBL — SAP Cloud Migration
- **Source:** [Google Cloud customer story](https://cloud.google.com/customers/southwire) · [Avvale case study](https://www.avvale.com/case-studies/southwire-sap-google-cloud-migration) · [Google Cloud blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/sap-google-cloud/why-southwire-migrated-to-google-cloud) · [Southwire announcement](https://www.southwire.com/blogs/southwire-migrates-sap-google)
- **Description:** Southwire upgraded SAP ECC to Enhancement Package 8, deployed SAP Business Warehouse on SAP HANA, upgraded SAP Process Orchestration, and migrated the SAP estate to Google Cloud with Avvale and implementation partner NIMBL, supported by the Google Cloud Acceleration Program.
- **Metrics:** Three SAP systems down only ~22 hours total during a planned July 4th maintenance-weekend cutover; post-migration system changes that previously required ≥1 hour of SAP downtime now cause no service interruption; keeps 30+ factories running 24/7.
- **Value proposition:** Near-zero platform downtime, five layers of data protection, technology refresh and performance boost, and a foundation for connected-factory and AI/ML initiatives on Google Cloud.

### 2. SAP — Customer Hub Self-Service Portal (SAP Commerce Cloud)
- **Source:** [SAP customer story (PDF/HTML)](https://www.sap.com/asset/dynamic/2025/05/405f8320-067f-0010-bca6-c68f7e60039b.html) · [ASUG interview](https://www.asug.com/insights/my-view-my-voice-investing-in-the-customer-experience-southwires-digitalization-journey)
- **Description:** As part of a "digital-first" transformation, Southwire built its Customer Hub self-service portal on SAP Commerce Cloud, integrated with SAP ERP and SAP Business Technology Platform, giving customers real-time order tracking, product information (including variant configuration and pricing), and self-service fulfillment.
- **Metrics:** Freed customer-service teams from responding to hundreds of thousands of internet queries; customers get real-time information 24/7 from office or job site; features can be added with quick turnarounds.
- **Value proposition:** Removes service bottlenecks — customers help themselves anytime, while service staff focus on higher-value customer work; flexible platform evolves with customer needs.

### 3. Rockwell Automation — "Southwire Makes the Right Connections" (Connected Enterprise)
- **Source:** [Rockwell Automation case study](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/company/news/case-studies/southwire-digital-transformation.html)
- **Description:** Multi-year Connected Enterprise / IT-OT convergence initiative connecting all of Southwire's insulated and bare wire-and-cable manufacturing, starting with a proof of concept and a two-plant pilot.
- **Metrics:** ~3-year rollout plan to connect all manufacturing facilities; operators use device-level quality data to verify correct settings on every run (published page emphasizes qualitative outcomes).
- **Value proposition:** Data-driven decision-making instead of opinion-driven; measure and manage productivity in new ways; integrated automation opens the door to further productivity innovations.

### 4. Crunchr — People Analytics for HR ("Igniting HR Operations at Southwire")
- **Source:** [Crunchr customer story](https://www.crunchr.com/customers/southwire/)
- **Description:** Southwire's HR team faced scattered, unreliable workforce data. It adopted Crunchr's people-analytics platform to centralize workforce insights in support of its growth agenda.
- **Metrics:** Platform integrated within about one month — significantly faster than the internal-build timeline quoted by Southwire's own BI team.
- **Value proposition:** Faster, more consistent workforce insights; enhanced decision-making and elevated HR strategy; strong vendor support versus a slower internal build.

### 7. Federated Media — Recruitment Marketing (Bremen, IN plant)

- **Value proposition:** Fast, high-volume hourly recruitment for a new plant using targeted local media.

## Part 2 — Case studies published by Southwire (its own products/services)

Main index: [Southwire Utility Whitepapers & Case Studies](https://www.southwire.com/Utility-Whitepapers-CaseStudy)

### 11. C7® Overhead Conductor — River Crossing
- **Source:** [C7 Overhead River Crossing Case Study (PDF)](https://www.southwire.com/Utility-Whitepapers-CaseStudy) · [Utility Dive sponsored article](https://www.utilitydive.com/spons/how-composite-core-conductors-reduce-the-costs-of-transmission-projects/621481/)
- **Description:** Riverbank erosion forced a transmission support structure to move, increasing a river-crossing span from 1,290 ft to 1,840 ft. ACSR would have sagged too much, requiring larger structures and foundations on both banks. The utility used Southwire's C7 carbon-fiber composite-core conductor instead.
- **Metrics:** Span increased ~43% (1,290 → 1,840 ft) with no increase in sag; avoided a full line rebuild and new foundations; C7 offers 40+ year service-life expectancy and single-failure-tolerant multi-stranded composite core.
- **Value proposition:** Higher capacity and superior sag/tension performance lets utilities reuse existing structures and cut total project cost.

### 12. Novinium® Underground Services — Salt River Project (cable rejuvenation)
- **Source:** [Salt River Project Whitepaper (PDF, southwire.com)](https://www.southwire.com/Utility-Whitepapers-CaseStudy)
- **Description:** Salt River Project (utility serving south Phoenix, AZ) had aging feeder and URD cable with rising maintenance costs and chose Novinium cable rejuvenation instead of replacement.
- **Metrics:** Less than 1% post-rejuvenation failure rate.
- **Value proposition:** Rejuvenation extends cable life at a fraction of replacement cost with minimal customer disruption.

### 13. Novinium® Modular Injection Component (MIC) — Mountain View Electric Association
- **Source:** [MIC Case Study (PDF, southwire.com)](https://www.southwire.com/Utility-Whitepapers-CaseStudy)
- **Description:** MVEA (Colorado co-op; 50,000+ members, 5,000 sq mi, ~600 miles of aging unjacketed XLPE URD cable) used the MIC — the first 200A accessory enabling sustained-pressure rejuvenation injection of energized medium-voltage cables — developed with Richards Manufacturing.
- **Metrics:** Full rejuvenation of radial segments completed within the standard 2-hour outage window normally reserved just for testing; improved crew productivity (crews move to the next segment while injection continues).
- **Value proposition:** Energized injection means shorter outages, higher crew productivity, and less rate-payer disruption than replacement or de-energized rejuvenation.

### 14. Novinium® Underground Services — Dayton Case Study
- **Source:** [Dayton Case Study (PDF, southwire.com)](https://www.southwire.com/Utility-Whitepapers-CaseStudy)
- **Description:** Additional Novinium underground cable-rejuvenation deployment (Dayton); companion to the SRP and MIC studies.
- **Metrics:** See PDF.
- **Value proposition:** Same rejuvenate-vs-replace economics applied at another utility.

### 15. Digital Grid Resiliency Solutions — Northeast US Investor-Owned Utility
- **Source:** [Digital Grid Resiliency Assessments](https://www.southwire.com/services/digital-grid-resiliency-assessments) · "Volatile to Resilient" case study PDF on the [whitepapers index](https://www.southwire.com/Utility-Whitepapers-CaseStudy) · [Utility Dive sponsored article](https://www.utilitydive.com/spons/how-software-and-ai-bolster-grid-resilience/723987/)
- **Description:** A Northeast US investor-owned utility worked with Southwire over several years, evaluating circuits and applying an enhanced grid-resiliency program to a subset of them. Southwire's AI-assisted assessment mines existing outage and GIS data to classify outage root causes at the device level.
- **Metrics:** Assessment identifies reliability improvements in as little as 30 days; prescriptive recommendations include cost justification and expected reliability gains.
- **Value proposition:** Data/AI-driven, device-level targeting of grid hardening spend instead of blanket programs.
