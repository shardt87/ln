01Definition & learnings

IPPs gave us a starting point. The owners pointed us at the EPCs.

The assignment was to evaluate the independent power producer pipeline. The owners taught us two things: EPCs write the cable specification, and OEM packages are where that specification is still open in 2026.

Where we started

Evaluate the IPP gas-plant pipeline

Understand owner priorities, pain points and engagement strategy with Business Development. Test assumptions on HV underground opportunities.

Work completed

A funnel, the relationships, the map

Built an internal project funnel of 17 pursuits, pursued owner conversations and mapped EPC relationships, plant applications, stakeholders and buying preference.

What changed

The whole plant, near to long term

  1. Owner discussions pointed to EPCs as the primary audience for influencing cable specifications and preference.
  2. An OEM execution strategy expands reach across modular power, packagers and integrators.

Working definition

Any entity that generates or stores electricity and delivers it to grid operators or end consumers

Conventional, renewable, nuclear and battery. Target audiences: generation asset owners and operators, design firms, EPCs, and generation equipment and component manufacturers. Initial focus: gas generation, engines, fuel cells and modular packages.

In scopeGeneration assets and electrical balance of plant

Including behind-the-meter generation. The rule follows the generation function, not the site.

Not in scopeUtility T&D, data-center campuses, industrial manufacturing

Existing renewables and storage teams keep separate commercial lanes. Nuclear is a later workstream.

02Where cable lives

Four families run the whole site. Two scopes buy them.

Cable is in every zone, under one percent of plant cost, at the end of every contract branch. That is why the play is the specification and the package, not the reel.

Inside the package, between the packages

The OEM may supply wiring inside a package up to terminal boxes or local panels. The EPC or an assigned installer supplies interconnects between packages. The exact terminal point follows the contract and the scope split. Two cable scopes, two buyers, two specifications.

06Solutions & value

The value proposition is how we build the moat, not what sits inside it

Candidates by lane, each with the product underneath and the evidence we have or still need. Electrical work that arrives ready for the installation plan.

OEM / EP&ES

  • Repeatable designs, fewer assembly hours. Standard specifications and qualified assemblies shipped as a finished EP&ES solution.
  • Products ahead of the trend. A 2.4 kV rating on standard cable ahead of 800 VDC, so customers do not jump to 5 kV.
  • A direct path when it qualifies. Jeff's team vets direct; distribution otherwise.

Evidence$9.7M quoted in July; Enchanted Rock $2.3M at ~40% quoted margin, order status to confirm.

EPC

  • Less field work. Factory-built, tested cable spine and terminations instead of site labor.
  • Material readiness. Standard put-ups, kitting, labeling and staged deliveries, run with distribution (CIE), not around it.
  • Engineering before the spec. The plant model and the one-line read: we reach the specification before it is written.

EvidenceCIE, Sept 2: fit, put-ups, visibility and delivery are what they get paid for; $24–28M directional across four Gemma opportunities. Fuel-cell spine precedent ~80% less field labor, internal figure to confirm.

IOU / utility generation

  • Predictable electrical delivery. Early planning and engineering support around the outage and energization dates.
  • The product the spec asks for. Thermoset CPE medium-voltage cable where the utility specifies it; HV underground and grounding where the project calls for it.

EvidenceTBD. Say it plainly; thermoset CPE is the lesson.

IPP / owner

  • Lower exposure at the electrical milestones. Owner requirements aligned with the EPC and OEM packages before procurement locks.
  • A leave-behind their engineers use. The plant model as the door-opener; the spine as the primary opportunity.

EvidenceNRG leave-behind forwarded to their generation team; Southern Power came to us.
