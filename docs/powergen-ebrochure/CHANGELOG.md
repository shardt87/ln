# Change log

PowerGen e-brochure, "Accelerating Time to Power". Consolidation of the September 2026 strategy update and the PowerGen email brochure into one customer package.

## Consolidated

- **Three levels instead of one long document.** Level 1: a nine-spread customer story (e-brochure sections A–H and the email PDF). Level 2: the interactive plant map with a panel per zone. Level 3: the filterable catalog and the technical appendix. The email brochure's five catalog pages and four zone-package pages moved out of the story and into levels 2 and 3.
- **Catalog organised into seven families** (MV power; LV distribution and motor feeders; VFD; control and instrumentation; flexible, portable and approved DC; grounding and bonding; communications, building systems and specialty). The source split the same references across "Power, VFD & DC 1–2", "Control, instrumentation & protection 1–2" and "Grounding, networks & specialty".
- **Duplicate references collapsed.** Spec 45059 appeared six times in the source (five stock numbers plus repeats); it is one specification linked from each stock number. 59548199 appeared on page 5, 10 and 11; it is one entry. 55052299 / 55050899 / 89008499 / 67747299 appeared under zones 05 and 16 and in the catalog; they are one entry each, tagged with both zones.
- **Zone packages preserved verbatim** (16 zones, source numbering and names, package name, cable content, package focus, specifies-and-buys, references).
- **Services** from pages 15–16 merged into one specification-to-installation sequence with the phase each service belongs to.
- **Case studies:** three featured, three in the library; each now shows problem, contribution, published result, proposed PowerGen application and the source PDF.

## Removed from the customer piece

- All internal strategy content from the deck: market sizing ($750M / $1.17B, GW pipeline), buyer-segment probabilities, path-to-market lanes with named Southwire staff, value-proposition candidates with quoted margins, leadership asks, "flagged bookings" and funnel figures. These are internal planning numbers and do not belong in a customer brochure.
- The race-car cover art, the state heat map, the lifecycle friction infographic (its percentages are internal publication figures without a customer-facing source link).
- The "Open online brochure" links on every page, replaced by the package's own navigation; the three films remain linked once, in the plant section.
- Repeated "Click a stock reference…" and "Confirm voltage…" footers, kept once each where they apply.

## Corrected or clarified

- **Reference types labelled** (product family, specification number, published base code, stock number, project-specific selection) and "stock numbers do not indicate inventory" stated once per catalog view.
- **Application boundaries stated explicitly** in the offering section: OEM internal wiring vs. field interconnection; fixed wiring vs. portable cable; MV insulation level; VFD duty; hazardous-location systems; immersion and circuit integrity; BESS DC vs. solar PV.
- **Scope statement**: generation scope (to the GSU) vs. downstream facility scope vs. grid interface, so behind-the-meter generation and campus distribution are not conflated.
- **Model disclaimer** carried on the map, the films and the PDF: illustrative, not a validated engineering design or digital twin; shared routes and staging add no circuit footage; do not carry model quantities into procurement.
- **Typography** set in the actual source faces (Nimbus Sans / Nimbus Sans Narrow Bold) instead of a substitute.
- The word "Open PDF" replaced by "Spec NNNNN · PDF" so the reader sees which document they are opening.
